# UCC Events

## Upcoming Events

### Open Day
UCC regularly holds open days for prospective students to learn more about programmes and facilities. Check the official website for dates.

### Information Sessions
Online and in-person information sessions are held for prospective students. These sessions provide details about programmes, admissions, and student life.

### Short Course Intakes
Professional short courses have multiple intakes throughout the year. Check the UCC website for the latest intake schedules.

### Career Fairs
UCC participates in and organises career fairs connecting students with potential employers.

For the latest event information, visit the official UCC website.
