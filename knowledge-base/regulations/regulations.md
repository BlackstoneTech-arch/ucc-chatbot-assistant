# UCC Regulations

## Academic Regulations

### General Regulations
Students are expected to adhere to the academic regulations of UCC and the University of Dar es Salaam.

### Examination Regulations
- Students must be registered for a course to sit for its examination
- Academic misconduct (plagiarism, cheating) is strictly prohibited
- Results are final unless an appeal is lodged within the specified period

### Programme Requirements
- Students must complete all required courses
- Minimum GPA requirements apply for progression
- Time limits apply for programme completion

### Code of Conduct
Students are expected to maintain high standards of conduct and professionalism.

For the complete and authoritative regulations, contact the Academic Affairs office or refer to the official UCC student handbook.
