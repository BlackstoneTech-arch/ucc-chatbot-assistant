# UCC Registration

## Course Registration

### Registration Process
1. Log in to the UCC student portal
2. Navigate to the registration section
3. Select courses for the semester
4. Review your selection
5. Confirm registration

### Registration Periods
Registration periods are announced in the academic calendar. Students must register within the specified period.

### Prerequisites
Ensure you have:
- Cleared any outstanding fees
- Met all academic prerequisites for selected courses
- Obtained necessary approvals from your department

### Adding/Dropping Courses
Students may add or drop courses within the first two weeks of the semester, subject to academic regulations.

For registration issues, contact the Academic Affairs office or your department.
