# UCC Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

**Q: What is UCC?**
A: UCC stands for University of Dar es Salaam Computing Centre. It is a leading ICT training and services institution in Tanzania, affiliated with the University of Dar es Salaam.

**Q: Where is UCC located?**
A: UCC is located at the University of Dar es Salaam main campus in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. It also has a presence in Dodoma.

**Q: What is the official website?**
A: The official UCC website is https://ucc.co.tz/

**Q: What is the admission portal?**
A: The official UCC admission portal is https://admission.ucc.co.tz/

## Admissions

**Q: How can I apply to UCC?**
A: Visit the official admission portal at https://admission.ucc.co.tz/, create an account, select your preferred programme, fill in the application form, upload required documents, pay the application fee, and submit.

**Q: What programmes does UCC offer?**
A: UCC offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in Computer Science, Information Technology, and related fields. Visit the official website for the complete list.

**Q: What are the entry requirements?**
A: Entry requirements vary by programme. Generally, undergraduate programmes require two advanced level principal passes including Mathematics/Physics. Postgraduate programmes require a relevant Bachelor's degree.

## Fees

**Q: How much are the tuition fees?**
A: Tuition fees vary by programme, academic year, and intake. Contact the Finance office or visit the official website for the most current fee structure.

**Q: How do I pay my fees?**
A: Fees are typically paid through bank deposits or mobile money to official UCC accounts. Verify payment details with the Finance office.

## Student Services

**Q: How do I register for courses?**
A: Log in to the UCC student portal and navigate to the registration section. Select your courses and confirm registration.

**Q: How can I get ICT support?**
A: Email ict@ucc.co.tz or call +255 22 2410 003. You can also visit the ICT Support office during office hours.
