# UCC Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

**Q: What is UCC?**
A: UCC stands for University of Dar es Salaam Computing Centre. It is an Information and Communication Technology (ICT) company owned by the University of Dar es Salaam (UDSM), established in 1999. UCC is a leading ICT training, consulting, and software development institution in Tanzania.

**Q: Where is UCC located?**
A: UCC has two branches:
- **Main Office (Headquarters):** University of Dar es Salaam, Mlimani Road, P.O. Box 35062, Dar es Salaam (UDSM Mlimani Campus, Opp. NBC Bank)
- **Dodoma Branch:** Plot No. 113, Mathias Street, Miyuji, P.O. Box 2501, Dodoma (Capital Compound)

**Q: What is UCC's official website?**
A: https://ucc.co.tz/

**Q: What is the admission portal?**
A: https://admission.ucc.co.tz/

**Q: What is UCC's motto?**
A: "Excellence, Innovation and Technological Foresight"

**Q: What are UCC's core values?**
A: Professionalism, Integrity, Accountability, and Customer Focus

## Admissions

**Q: How can I apply to UCC?**
A: Visit https://admission.ucc.co.tz/, create an account, select your preferred programme, fill in the application form, upload required documents, and submit. All applications are online.

**Q: What programmes does UCC offer?**
A: UCC offers 4 academic programmes (DCIT, DBIT, CCIT, CBIT) and 24+ professional courses including PMP, CISA, CISM, ITIL, COBIT, and many more.

**Q: What are the entry requirements for Diploma programmes?**
A: 
- **Option A:** ACSEE with one (1) principal pass and one (1) subsidiary pass, OR
- **Option B:** Basic Technician Certificate (NTA Level 4) in Computer Science, IT, BIT, Computer Engineering, or Electronic Engineering

**Q: What are the entry requirements for Certificate programmes?**
A: Certificate of Secondary Education Examination (CSEE) with passes in relevant subjects.

**Q: When is the next intake?**
A: The latest invitation is for the October 2026/2027 intake for Certificate and Diploma programmes (announced June 2026).

## Fees

**Q: How much is the DCIT tuition fee?**
A: The total fee for DCIT is TZS 3,020,000, broken down as:
- Tuition fee: TZS 2,800,000
- Examination fee: TZS 60,000
- Identity card: TZS 20,000
- ICT Services fee: TZS 100,000
- NACTE Quality Assurance Fee: TZS 40,000

**Q: How much is the application fee?**
A: Application fees vary by programme. Check the admission portal for the current fee.

**Q: How do I pay my fees?**
A: Fees are paid directly to the University Computing Centre Limited. Contact the Finance office for payment details.

## Courses

**Q: What is the duration of the DCIT programme?**
A: The DCIT is a 2-year programme managed in 4 semesters.

**Q: What is the duration of Certificate programmes?**
A: Certificate programmes are typically 1 year.

**Q: Are there tailor-made courses available?**
A: Yes, UCC designs and delivers customised training programmes for organisations. Contact UCC for tailor-made course enquiries.

## Services

**Q: Does UCC offer IT consulting?**
A: Yes, UCC offers IT consulting services including Business Process Improvement, Enterprise Architecture, IT Strategic Planning, Disaster Recovery Planning, Tender Evaluations, and more.

**Q: Does UCC develop software?**
A: Yes, UCC has developed 6 major software products: ARIS, OLASS, IFMIS, eTac, HMS, and MES across education, financial, health, and M&E sectors.

**Q: Can I take international certification exams at UCC?**
A: Yes, UCC is an Authorised Pearson VUE Testing Centre where you can take Microsoft, Cisco, and other certification exams.
