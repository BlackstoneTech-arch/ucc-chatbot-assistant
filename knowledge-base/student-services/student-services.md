# UCC Student Services

## Student Services

### Registration
Students register for courses through the UCC student portal. The academic calendar indicates registration periods for each semester.

### Academic Calendar
The academic year typically consists of two semesters:
- Semester I: August - January
- Semester II: February - June

For the exact dates, refer to the official UCC academic calendar.

### Student Support Services
UCC provides various student support services including:
- Academic advising
- Career counselling
- Student welfare services
- Accommodation information
- Health services

### Examination Information
- Examinations are held at the end of each semester
- Examination results are published through the student portal
- Students must adhere to examination rules and regulations

### Graduation
Graduation ceremonies are held annually. Eligible students are notified through official channels.

For detailed student services information, contact the Student Services office.
