# UCC Fees Structure

## Diploma in Computing and Information Technology (DCIT) - Fee Structure

Money payable directly to the University Computing Centre Limited (In TZS):

| Item | Amount (TZS) |
|------|-------------:|
| Tuition fee | 2,800,000 |
| Examination fee | 60,000 |
| Identity card* | 20,000 |
| ICT Services fee | 100,000 |
| NACTE Quality Assurance Fee | 40,000 |
| **Total** | **3,020,000** |

\* Identity card fee is a one-time charge.

## Professional Courses
Professional certification courses (PMP, CISA, CISM, ITIL, COBIT, etc.) have separate fee structures. Contact UCC for the current pricing of each professional course.

## Payment Information
Fees are paid directly to the University Computing Centre Limited. Always verify payment details with the Finance office before making any payments.
