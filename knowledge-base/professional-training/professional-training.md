# UCC Professional Training

## Professional ICT Training

UCC offers a range of professional short courses and training programmes designed to meet industry needs.

### Available Professional Courses
- Software Development (Web, Mobile, Desktop)
- Network Administration and Security
- Database Administration
- Cloud Computing Fundamentals
- Data Science and Analytics
- Cybersecurity
- Digital Marketing
- Project Management

### Course Duration
Professional courses typically range from 4 weeks to 6 months, depending on the course.

### Entry Requirements
Vary by course. Basic computer literacy is typically required.

### Certification
Upon successful completion, participants receive a UCC certificate of completion.

### Registration
To register for a professional course, visit the UCC website or contact the Professional Training office.

For the latest course offerings, schedules, and fees, please visit the official UCC website.
