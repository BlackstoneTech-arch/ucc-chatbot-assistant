# UCC Programmes - Academic & Professional

## Academic Programmes

UCC offers the following academic programmes, aligned with NACTE and CCNA based curricula. These are practical-oriented, competence-based programmes designed to produce IT professionals ready for local and international industries.

### 1. Diploma in Computing and Information Technology (DCIT)
- **Category:** Academic
- **Duration:** 2 years (4 semesters)
- **Description:** Follows both NACTE and CCNA based curricula. Practical-oriented, competence-based programme that absorbs current technology developments, provides competitive academic outputs, and produces IT professionals ready for industry. Serves as a bridge for those aspiring for higher learning in ICT.
- **Entry Requirements:**
  - Advanced Certificate of Secondary Education Examination (ACSEE) with one (1) principal pass and one (1) subsidiary pass, OR
  - Basic Technician Certificate (NTA Level 4) in Computer Science (CS), Information Technology (IT), Business Information Technology (BIT), Computer Engineering, or Electronic Engineering.
- **Locations:** UCC HQ Branch at UDSM Mlimani Campus (Opp. NBC Bank) and Dodoma Branch (Capital Compound, Mathias Street, Miyuji)
- **Application:** https://admission.ucc.co.tz/

### 2. Diploma in Business Information Technology (DBIT)
- **Category:** Academic
- **Description:** Combines business management with information technology skills, preparing students for IT roles in business environments.
- **Entry Requirements:** Advanced Certificate of Secondary Education Examination (ACSEE) with one (1) principal pass and one (1) subsidiary pass, OR Basic Technician Certificate (NTA Level 4) in relevant fields.
- **Application:** https://admission.ucc.co.tz/

### 3. Certificate in Computing and Information Technology (CCIT)
- **Category:** Academic
- **Description:** Foundational programme for entry into the ICT field, providing essential computing and IT skills.
- **Entry Requirements:** Certificate of Secondary Education Examination (CSEE) with passes in relevant subjects.
- **Application:** https://admission.ucc.co.tz/

### 4. Certificate in Business Information Technology (CBIT)
- **Category:** Academic
- **Description:** Foundation programme combining basic business concepts with information technology skills.
- **Entry Requirements:** Certificate of Secondary Education Examination (CSEE) with passes in relevant subjects.
- **Application:** https://admission.ucc.co.tz/
