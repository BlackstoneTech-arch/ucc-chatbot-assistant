# UCC Programmes

## Available Programmes (2025/2026 - 2026/2027)

### Undergraduate Programmes

1. **Bachelor of Science in Computer Science** (BSc CS)
   - Duration: 3 years
   - Entry Requirements: Two advanced level principal passes in Mathematics/Physics/ Chemistry/Biology/Geography and one in any other subject. OR equivalent qualifications.
   - Category: Undergraduate
   - Academic Year: 2026/2027

2. **Bachelor of Science in Information Technology** (BSc IT)
   - Duration: 3 years
   - Entry Requirements: Two advanced level principal passes including Mathematics/Physics and one in any other subject.
   - Category: Undergraduate
   - Academic Year: 2026/2027

3. **Diploma in Computing and Information Technology** (DCIT)
   - Duration: 2 years
   - Entry Requirements: Certificate level or equivalent with relevant ICT/Computer Studies background.
   - Category: Diploma
   - Academic Year: 2026/2027

### Postgraduate Programmes

1. **Postgraduate Diploma in Information Technology** (PGDIT)
   - Duration: 1 year
   - Entry Requirements: Bachelor's degree in any discipline.
   - Category: Postgraduate
   - Academic Year: 2026/2027

2. **Master of Science in Information Technology** (MSc IT)
   - Duration: 2 years
   - Entry Requirements: Relevant Bachelor's degree (minimum Second Class Lower).
   - Category: Postgraduate
   - Academic Year: 2026/2027

### Professional Training

UCC also offers professional short courses in various ICT areas including:
- Software Development
- Network Administration
- Cybersecurity
- Database Management
- Web Development
- Data Science

For detailed and up-to-date programme information, visit the official UCC website or contact the admissions office.
