# UCC Software Products & Services

UCC develops and provides innovative software solutions to the education, financial, and health sectors across Tanzania and East Africa.

## Educational Systems

### 1. Academic Registration Information System (ARIS)
A comprehensive system for managing student academic records, course registration, and academic administration in higher learning institutions.

### 2. Online Application and Selection System (OLASS)
A web-based platform that automates the application and selection process for academic programmes, streamlining admissions for institutions.

## Financial Systems

### 3. Integrated Financial Management Information System (IFMIS)
A complete financial management solution covering budgeting, accounting, revenue collection, expenditure management, and financial reporting.

### 4. e-Ticketing and Access Control System (eTac)
An electronic ticketing and access control solution for managing entry, ticketing, and access in various venues and facilities.

## Health Systems

### 5. Hospital Management System (HMS)
A comprehensive hospital management solution covering patient registration, appointment scheduling, clinical records, pharmacy, laboratory, billing, and reporting.

## Monitoring & Evaluation Systems

### 6. Monitoring and Evaluation System (MES)
A robust M&E system for tracking project activities, indicators, outputs, outcomes, and impact across development programmes.
