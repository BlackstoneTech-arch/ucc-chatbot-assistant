# UCC About - University of Dar es Salaam Computing Centre

## Overview

The University of Dar es Salaam Computing Centre (UCC) is an Information and Communication Technology (ICT) Company owned by the University of Dar es Salaam (UDSM). UCC was established in 1999. UCC headquarter is located at UDSM's Julius Kambarage Nyerere Mlimani Campus. To ensure greater outreach to Tanzanians, UCC is having a branch in Dodoma, the Capital City of Tanzania.

## Our Vision
To become a regionally recognized ICT center of excellence.

## Our Mission
To lead in the innovation and development of the most advanced ICT products and services that contribute to the social-economic development in the region.

## Our Services

### 1. IT Training Services
UCC offers training services designed hand in hand with current technology. Training categories include:
- **Academic Programmes** - Certificate and Diploma programmes
- **Professional Courses** - Industry certifications (PMP, CISA, CISM, ITIL, COBIT, etc.)
- **Tailor-Made Courses** - Customised training for organisations

### 2. IT Infrastructure
- IT Infrastructure design, installation, and management
- Data Hosting, Co-location and Cloud Services
- IT Security solutions
- IT Managed Services
- Domain Registration and Web Hosting

### 3. Software Products & Services
UCC develops and provides the following software solutions:
- **Educational Systems:** Academic Registration Information System (ARIS), Online Application and Selection System (OLASS)
- **Financial Systems:** Integrated Financial Management Information System (IFMIS), e-Ticketing and Access Control System (eTac)
- **Health Systems:** Hospital Management System (HMS)
- **M&E Systems:** Monitoring and Evaluation System (MES)

### 4. IT Consulting
- Business Process Improvement
- Quality and Performance Management
- Enterprise Architecture Development
- IT Strategic Planning and Policy Development
- Change Management and Knowledge Management
- IT Disaster Recovery and Business Continuity Planning
- Technical Specifications, Terms of Reference (ToR) and Tender Documents preparation
- Tender Evaluations
- IT Project Management
- Staff Recruitment

### 5. Professional Qualification Testing Service
UCC is an Authorized Pearson VUE Testing Centre dedicated to enabling professionals and academics pursuing their online examinations. Examinations include:
- Microsoft Certification (MCSA, MCSE, MCDA)
- Cisco Certification (CCNA, CCNP, CCIP)
