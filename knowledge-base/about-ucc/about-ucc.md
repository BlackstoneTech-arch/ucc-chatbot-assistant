# UCC About

## University of Dar es Salaam Computing Centre (UCC)

UCC is a leading ICT training and services institution in Tanzania, affiliated with the University of Dar es Salaam.

### Our Mission
To provide quality ICT education, training, and services that meet national and international standards.

### Our Vision
To be a centre of excellence in ICT education and services in East Africa.

### Campuses
UCC operates from the main campus in Dar es Salaam and has a presence in Dodoma.

### History
UCC has a long history of providing ICT education and training in Tanzania, producing thousands of skilled ICT professionals.
