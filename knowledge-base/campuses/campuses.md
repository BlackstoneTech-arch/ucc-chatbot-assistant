# UCC Campuses and Locations

## Campuses

### Main Campus - Dar es Salaam
The main campus of UCC is located at the University of Dar es Salaam main campus in Dar es Salaam.

**Address:**
University of Dar es Salaam Computing Centre (UCC)
P.O. Box 35091
Dar es Salaam, Tanzania

**Coordinates:** Approximately 6.8°S, 39.3°E

### Dodoma Centre
UCC also has a presence in Dodoma, the capital city of Tanzania.

For the most current campus information, addresses, and directions, please visit the official UCC website at https://ucc.co.tz/.
