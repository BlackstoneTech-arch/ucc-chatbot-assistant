# UCC Accommodation

## Student Accommodation

### On-Campus Accommodation
UCC provides limited on-campus accommodation. Allocation is based on availability and specific criteria.

### Off-Campus Accommodation
The majority of students live off-campus. UCC can provide information about nearby housing options.

### Accommodation Application
- On-campus accommodation applications are processed through the Student Services office
- Priority is given to first-year students and students with special needs
- Accommodation fees vary by room type and academic year

### Accommodation Requirements
Students staying in on-campus accommodation must:
- Adhere to residence rules and regulations
- Maintain cleanliness and order
- Respect other residents

For accommodation enquiries, contact the Student Services office.
