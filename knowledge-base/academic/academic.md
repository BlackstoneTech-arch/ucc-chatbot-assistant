# UCC Academic Information

## Academic Year Structure

The academic year typically consists of two semesters:
- **Semester I:** August - January
- **Semester II:** February - June

For exact dates, refer to the official UCC academic calendar.

## Programme Types

### Diploma Programmes (2 years, 4 semesters)
- **DCIT** — Diploma in Computing and Information Technology
- **DBIT** — Diploma in Business Information Technology

### Certificate Programmes (1 year, 2 semesters)
- **CCIT** — Certificate in Computing and Information Technology
- **CBIT** — Certificate in Business Information Technology

## Entry Qualifications

### Diploma (DCIT, DBIT)
- ACSEE with at least 1 principal pass and 1 subsidiary pass, OR
- Basic Technician Certificate (NTA Level 4) in a relevant field

### Certificate (CCIT, CBIT)
- CSEE with at least 4 passes in non-religious subjects, OR
- National Vocational Training Award Level III (Trade Test Grade I)

## Course Registration

1. Log in to the UCC student portal
2. Navigate to the registration section
3. Select courses for the semester
4. Review your selection
5. Confirm registration

Registration periods are announced in the academic calendar. Students must register within the specified period.

## Examinations

- Examinations are conducted at the end of each semester
- The examination timetable is published before the examination period
- Students must carry valid identification to examinations
- Results are published through the official student portal

## Important Notes

- The academic calendar and timetables are **not** published online — only distributed to registered students via the student portal / notice boards. Never invent dates for these.
- For the complete and authoritative academic regulations, contact the Academic Affairs office or refer to the official UCC student handbook.
