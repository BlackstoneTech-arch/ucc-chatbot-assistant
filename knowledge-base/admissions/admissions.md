# UCC Admissions

## Admission Information

### Application Process
1. Visit the official admission portal at https://admission.ucc.co.tz/
2. Create an account or log in
3. Select your preferred programme
4. Fill in the application form
5. Upload required documents
6. Pay the application fee
7. Submit the application

### Application Deadlines
Application deadlines vary by programme and intake. Please check the official UCC admission portal for current deadlines.

### Required Documents
- Completed application form
- Academic certificates and transcripts
- Birth certificate or national ID
- Passport-sized photographs
- Application fee payment receipt

### Application Fee
Application fees vary by programme. Please check the admission portal for the current fee structure.

### Admission Status
After submitting your application, you can check your admission status through the official admission portal.

For verified and up-to-date admission information, always refer to the official UCC admission portal.
