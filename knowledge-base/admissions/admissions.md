# UCC Admissions

## Current Intake: October 2026/2027
UCC has issued an invitation to apply for admission into Certificate and Diploma programmes for the October 2026/2027 intake.

## Application Portal
All eligible applicants are required to apply online through the official admission portal:
**https://admission.ucc.co.tz/**

## Application Process
1. Visit the official admission portal at https://admission.ucc.co.tz/
2. Create an account or log in
3. Select your preferred programme (Certificate or Diploma)
4. Fill in the application form completely
5. Upload all required documents
6. Pay the application fee
7. Submit the application
8. Track your application status through the portal

## Available Programmes for Admission
- **Diploma in Computing and Information Technology (DCIT)**
- **Diploma in Business Information Technology (DBIT)**
- **Certificate in Computing and Information Technology (CCIT)**
- **Certificate in Business Information Technology (CBIT)**

## Entry Qualifications

### For Diploma Programmes (DCIT, DBIT)
**Option A:**
- Advanced Certificate of Secondary Education Examination (ACSEE) with one (1) principal pass and one (1) subsidiary pass

**Option B:**
- Basic Technician Certificate (NTA Level 4) in Computer Science (CS), Information Technology (IT), Business Information Technology (BIT), Computer Engineering, or Electronic Engineering

### For Certificate Programmes (CCIT, CBIT)
- Certificate of Secondary Education Examination (CSEE) with passes in relevant subjects

## Required Documents
- Completed online application form
- Academic certificates and transcripts
- Birth certificate or national ID
- Passport-sized photographs
- Application fee payment receipt
