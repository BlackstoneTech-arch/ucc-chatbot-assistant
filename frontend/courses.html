Verified Academic Programmes (2026/2027)

The following programmes are currently listed on the official UCC website. For professional and short courses, please contact UCC directly.

Diploma in Computing and Information Technology (DCIT)

Duration: 2 years (4 semesters)

Entry Requirements: ACSEE with at least 1 principal pass and 1 subsidiary pass; OR Basic Technician Certificate (NTA Level 4) in Computer Science, Information Technology, Business Information Technology, Computer Engineering, or Electronic Engineering.

Fees (2026/2027): Tuition TZS 2,800,000, Examination TZS 60,000, ID Card TZS 20,000, ICT Services TZS 100,000, NACTE Quality Assurance TZS 40,000. Total: TZS 3,020,000.

Locations: UCC Headquarters at UDSM Mlimani Campus opposite NBC Bank; UCC Dodoma Branch at Capital Compound, Mathias Street, Miyuji.

Diploma in Business Information Technology (DBIT)

Duration: 2 years

Entry Requirements: ACSEE with at least 1 principal pass and 1 subsidiary pass; OR Basic Technician Certificate (NTA Level 4) in Business Administration, Accountancy, Computer Science, Information Technology, Business Information Technology, or Computer Engineering.

Fees (2026/2027): Tuition TZS 2,800,000, Examination TZS 60,000, ID Card TZS 20,000, ICT Services TZS 100,000, NACTE Quality Assurance TZS 40,000. Total: TZS 3,020,000.

Locations: UCC Headquarters at UDSM Mlimani Campus opposite NBC Bank; UCC Dodoma Branch at Capital Compound, Mathias Street, Miyuji.

Certificate in Computing and Information Technology (CCIT)

Duration: Varies by programme

Entry Requirements: Certificate of Secondary Education with at least 4 passes in non-religious subjects; OR National Vocational Training Award Level III (Trade Test Grade I) from a recognized institution.

Fees (2026/2027): Tuition TZS 1,200,000, Examination TZS 30,000, ID Card TZS 20,000, ICT Services TZS 100,000, NACTE Quality Assurance TZS 20,000. Total: TZS 1,370,000.

Locations: UCC Headquarters at UDSM Mlimani Campus opposite NBC Bank; UCC Dodoma Branch at Capital Compound, Mathias Street, Miyuji.
