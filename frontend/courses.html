Programmes and Courses

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSc CS)

Duration: 3 years. Entry Requirements: Two advanced level principal passes including Mathematics/Physics.

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSc IT)

Duration: 3 years. Entry Requirements: Two advanced level principal passes including Mathematics/Physics.

Diploma in Computing and Information Technology (DCIT)

Duration: 2 years. Entry Requirements: Certificate level or equivalent with relevant ICT background.

Postgraduate Diploma in IT (PGDIT)

Duration: 1 year. Entry Requirements: Bachelor's degree in any discipline.

Master of Science in IT (MSc IT)

Duration: 2 years. Entry Requirements: Relevant Bachelor's degree (minimum Second Class Lower).
