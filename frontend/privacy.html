Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the University Computing Centre (UCC) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the UCC Customer Care Assistant ("the Chatbot"). By using the Chatbot you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

  • Conversation content: the questions you submit and the responses you receive. Messages are stored on secure servers in order to improve service quality and provide continuity if you return within the same session.
  • Technical metadata: a randomly generated session identifier, your browser type, and your IP address. This data is used for security, abuse prevention, and aggregated analytics.
  • Voluntary feedback: if you rate an answer (thumbs up/down) or leave a comment, that feedback is stored with the related conversation.

2. How we use your information

  • To answer your questions about UCC programmes, admissions, fees, and services.
  • To improve the accuracy and coverage of the Chatbot's knowledge base.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, security incidents, and service disruptions.
  • To comply with University of Dar es Salaam and Government of Tanzania record-keeping obligations.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not require you to register or log in to use the Chatbot.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or physical address unless you voluntarily provide them inside a message.
  • We do not place advertising cookies or share data with third-party advertisers.

4. Data retention

Conversation records are retained for a maximum of 24 months unless required to be kept longer for audit or legal reasons. After this period they are deleted or fully anonymized.

5. Your rights

Under the Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act and similar frameworks, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@ucc.co.tz.

6. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards including HTTPS encryption in transit, encrypted storage of any personal data, and strict role-based access controls for staff who can read conversation records.

7. Children's privacy

The Chatbot is intended for prospective students, applicants, parents, and the general public. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 outside the lawful university admission process.

8. AI-generated content

The Chatbot is an AI customer-care assistant. Responses are generated from a curated knowledge base of verified UCC information. While we work hard to keep the information accurate and up to date, the Chatbot may occasionally produce incorrect or incomplete answers. For any decision that depends on programme details, fees, or admission requirements, please confirm directly with UCC by emailing admissions@ucc.co.tz or calling +255 22 2410641/5.

9. Contact

For any privacy question or complaint, contact the UCC Data Protection Officer at info@ucc.co.tz or +255 22 2410641/5.
