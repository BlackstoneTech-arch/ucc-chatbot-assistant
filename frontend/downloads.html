How to apply — step by step

  1. Download a programme brochure above (DCIT, DBIT, CCIT, or CBIT) and read it carefully.
  2. Create an account at admission.ucc.co.tz using your email and phone number. Activate via the link sent to your email.
  3. Pay the application fee: TZS 15,000 for local applicants, TZS 30,000 for foreign applicants. Use the reference number from the admission portal via M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa (Mix by Yas), or Airtel Money (Malipo ya Serikali → Reference no). Foreign applicants pay by SWIFT to CRDB Bank (code: CORUTZTZ, A/C: 02J1020063300).
  4. Complete the 4-step form: My Profile → Application Fee Payment → Academic Qualification → Programme Choices. Add your CSEE / ACSEE index numbers (or upload foreign certificates + NECTA equivalence letter) and rank your programme choices.
  5. Submit and await selection. Successful applicants will be notified by SMS / email and must report in November 2026 for the October 2026/2027 intake.
