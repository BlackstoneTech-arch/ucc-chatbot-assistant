Cookie Notice

Last updated: 3 September 2026

The UCC Customer Care Assistant ("the Chatbot") uses only a small amount of browser storage. We do not set any tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies. Below is a complete list of what is stored on your device.

1. Local storage (localStorage)

KeyPurposeRetention
ucc_chat_sessionRandom session identifier so the same conversation can be continued if you refresh the page.7 days, or until you click "Start new conversation".
ucc_chat_historyThe text of your last conversation so you can scroll back through it after a page refresh.7 days, or until you click "Start new conversation".
ucc_chat_langYour selected or detected language (English / Kiswahili).1 year.

2. No third-party cookies

The Chatbot does not embed any third-party trackers, advertising networks, or social-media pixels. We may at some point enable privacy-respecting analytics (e.g. Plausible or Umami self-hosted) — if we do, this page will be updated and you will be informed.

3. How to clear stored data

You can clear everything we store at any time by clicking "Start new conversation" in the chat, or by clearing site data in your browser settings. The Chatbot will continue to work after clearing.
